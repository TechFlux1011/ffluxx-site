The name

Flux

Nothing worth making sits still. Software and games pull in different directions — different tools, different audiences, different rhythms — and ffluxx exists to hold both without pretending they're the same thing.

The mark is two waves, mirrored. They run apart, cross, run apart again, and cross once more: two lines, two X's, one word. Range, not restlessness.
