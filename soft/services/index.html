How it works

Five steps · no surprises

Most of what goes wrong on a small software project goes wrong before anyone writes code — unclear scope, a moving finish line, a price that changes after you've committed. This is how we avoid that.

01

A conversation

Twenty minutes about what you need and why. Free, and there's no pitch at the end of it.

02

A written scope

Exactly what's being built, what it costs, and when it's done. You approve it before anything begins.

03

Design before build

You see the thing before it's made, and change it while changing it is cheap. Two rounds included.

04

Built in the open

A live link from the first week. You watch it come together instead of waiting for a reveal.

05

Handover

Everything transfers to accounts in your name. Leave whenever you like — nothing is held hostage.
